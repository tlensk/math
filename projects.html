COMPUTATIONAL GENOMICS · STRUCTURAL INFERENCE

Conserved architecture in diverse phage genomes

Exploring a conserved distal-tail helical extension associated with tailspike attachment in Gram-negative siphophages.

Developed and applied an integrated discovery workflow combining sequence profiles, large-scale similarity search, genomic organization, structural comparison, and systematic manual curation. The screening covered 5,167 complete phage genomes and 541,986 proteins.

Transferable approach

Inferring modular organization from heterogeneous evidence; designing computational screens; evaluating false positives; distinguishing conserved structure from artifacts.

NEURAL NETWORKS · MULTI-OBJECTIVE OPTIMIZATION

Personalized cardiovascular treatment planning

Integrating predictive neural-network models with constrained optimization for individualized statin treatment decisions.

The published framework analyzed 38,214 patients and used 70 predictors to estimate risks of adverse symptoms and medication discontinuation across treatment alternatives. My documented author contributions included software, supervision, validation, and visualization.

Transferable approach

Model evaluation, scientific software development, validation, and understanding how complex predictive information can support transparent decision processes.

03 / 2017PUBLISHED RESEARCH

LONGITUDINAL PREDICTION · FEATURE SELECTION

Sequential modeling of cognitive decline

Predicting changes in cognitive function using repeated observations and models that incorporate time-varying risk factors.

The study analyzed 1,370 participants and 6,785 short-interval records. It selected 15 predictors from more than 200 candidates and constructed sequential prediction units using linear regression following comparisons with other predictive methods.

Transferable approach

Feature selection, temporal modeling, quantitative assessment of prediction error, and explicit testing of how new information improves a model.

ALGORITHMS · INTERACTIVE VISUALIZATION

Pairwise Sequence Similarity Heatmap

An interactive browser-based tool for working with large pairwise sequence-comparison datasets.

The application supports identity, coverage, and bit-score analysis, local and global comparison, hierarchical and nearest-neighbor organization, and export of results. It translates high-dimensional similarity patterns into inspectable visual structure.

Transferable approach

Scientific software engineering, matrix data, algorithmic visualization, and exploratory analysis of structured relationships.
